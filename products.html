What We Manufacture

One Factory, Ten Product Lines, One Standard

Since 1977, Jai Baba Nanak Cloth Trading Co. has manufactured, supplied, wholesaled, traded and exported interlining fabrics for garment makers across India and abroad. Every category below is produced on our own line and inspected before dispatch, so the roll you reorder performs exactly like the one you first sampled. Browse the range, compare categories against your application, or go straight to our quality standards and garment applications pages for more detail.

Structured

Buckram Fabric

A firm, stiff sew-in fabric that holds its shape without heat-fusing — used to structure caps, belts, bag panels and other rigid garment parts.

Heat-Bonded

Fusible Interlining

A resin-coated fabric that heat-bonds to the outer shell for a clean, bubble-free fuse — the standard choice for collars, cuffs and plackets.

View Details
Durable

Pocketing Cloth

A tightly woven, hard-wearing cloth built for trouser, jeans and jacket pockets — made to outlast the outer garment through repeated wash cycles.

Identify Your Garment Application

2

Confirm Width & Quantity Needed
