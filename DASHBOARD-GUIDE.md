# Your website dashboard: a short guide

Everything on www.jaibabainterlinings.com can be changed here without a web developer: texts, pictures, questions and answers, phone numbers, the menu and the footer.

**Open it:** go to **https://www.jaibabainterlinings.com/admin/** and type your password.
For safety, after 5 wrong passwords the login waits 15 minutes.

---

## The screens (menu on the left)
| Screen | Use it to |
|---|---|
| **Pages** | find a page and click **Edit** |
| **Pictures** | upload new photos and find the ones on the site |
| **Google check** | see what to improve so Google shows your pages well |
| **Redirects** | send an old web address to a new page |
| **Settings** | phone numbers, email, addresses, WhatsApp, social media, logo, menu, footer |
| **Trash** | bring back a page you deleted |

## Change a page
1. **Pages** → click **Edit** next to the page.
2. The page is made of **sections**, from top to bottom: the top banner, picture with text, cards, a table, questions & answers, product cards, the dark "call to action" banner. Click a section to open it.
3. Change the text in the boxes. The text boxes have a small toolbar: **B** (bold), *I* (italic), link, list.
4. Click **Preview changes** to see the page in a new tab before anyone else does.
5. Click **Save changes**. The page on the website changes at once.

**Other things you can do with sections**
- **Move:** use the ↑ ↓ arrows.
- **Hide for a while:** tick "Hide this section on the website".
- **Remove:** the bin icon.
- **Add:** "Add a section" at the bottom.

**Pictures in a section**
- Click **Choose** next to a picture to pick one from your library, or upload a new one there.
- Always fill in **Picture description**: say in a few words what is in the photo. Google and visitors using screen readers rely on it.

**Things you rarely need** sit under "More options".

## Add a new product page
**Create new page** (top left) → give it a name → choose "A copy of an existing page" and pick a similar product. That keeps the same layout, so you only change the texts and pictures.
The new page is a **draft**: nobody sees it until you click **Publish**.

To show the new product on the Home and Products pages, open those pages, find the **Product cards** section, and add the product there.

## The "Google & sharing" tab (on every page)
- **Title in Google:** about 30–60 characters, with the most important words first. For example: "Buckram Fabric Manufacturer in Delhi | Jaibaba Interlinings".
- **Description in Google:** about 120–160 characters. Say what the buyer gets and why to contact you.
- **Main search phrase:** what a buyer would type into Google, e.g. "fusible interlining manufacturer". The check then tells you where to use it.
- The box at the top shows how the page may look in Google, and the counters turn green when the length is right.
- **Hide this page from Google:** only for pages that shouldn't appear in search (for example a thank-you page).

The side panel's **Quick Google check** updates while you type. **Full check** shows the deeper check from the last save, for example broken links or pictures without a description.

## Questions & answers
Write each answer starting with the direct answer, then the detail. For example: "Yes. We supply custom widths on bulk orders…". Google, ChatGPT and other AI tools can quote these answers.

## Made a mistake?
- **Earlier versions** (in the editor's side panel) lists the last 30 saves of the page. Click **Restore** on the one you want. The version you replaced is kept too, so you can always go back.
- A **deleted page** goes to the **Trash**: click **Restore** there.
- If someone saved the same page in another window, the dashboard won't let one save overwrite the other: it asks you to reload first.

## Changing a page's web address
On **Page settings**, change "Web address". The old address automatically sends visitors and Google to the new one, so you don't lose your Google ranking.

## Settings
- **Phone, email, addresses, opening hours, WhatsApp, social media:** change them once here and they change on every page.
- **Menu and footer:** add, rename or reorder links.
- **Facts for Google and AI search:** what the business does, the year it was founded, and the address in parts. Only enter things that are true.
- **Visitor statistics:** paste your Google Analytics or Meta Pixel ID here when you have one.
- **Change password:** at the bottom of Settings.

## Good habits
- Use real photos of your own products where you can, and always add a picture description.
- Don't promise numbers, certificates or clients you can't show. Google and buyers trust facts.
- Look at **Google check** once a month and fix the red points first.

## Help
- **Forgot the password:** your web developer can set a new one on the server (`php app/cli.php set-password`).
- **Backups:** a copy of all pages, settings and pictures is made every night, and the last 14 days are kept.
