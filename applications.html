Garment Applications

Seven Garment Categories, One Consistent Standard

Every garment category places a different demand on interlining — some need stiffness, some need wash durability, some need moulded shape. Here's how our product range maps to each.

Shirts

Shirts & Formalwear

Our readymade collar & cuff paired with fusible interlining gives a crisp collar roll and a cuff edge that stays sharp through repeated ironing and washing.

Uniforms

School & Corporate Uniforms

Fusible interlining and pocketing cloth are built to survive institutional laundering cycles without delaminating or losing shape.

Blazers

Blazers & Jackets

Shoulder pads combined with fusible interlining build the chest, lapel and shoulder structure a tailored blazer needs to hold its line.

Trousers

Trousers & Bottomwear

Pocketing cloth reinforces pocket bags against tearing, while belt roll & placket (patti) keeps the waist edge firm through daily wear.

Caps

Caps & Headwear

Buckram fabric gives the crown and peak the stiffness needed to hold shape wear after wear, wash after wash.

Industrial

Industrial Garments

Heavy-duty pocketing cloth and fusible interlining are specified where workwear faces daily abrasion, not occasional wear.

Fashion

Fashion & Design-Led Garments

Design-driven silhouettes draw on buckram, fusible interlining or EVA foam depending on how much structure the piece calls for.

Quick Reference

Application-To-Product Comparison Guide

A quick reference for procurement teams matching garment category to recommended product and the structural requirement it addresses.

Application Recommended Product Typical Requirement
Shirts & Formalwear Readymade Collar & Cuff + Fusible Interlining Crisp collar roll, sharp cuff edge, clean fuse with no bubbling
School & Corporate Uniforms Fusible Interlining + Pocketing Cloth Bond strength that holds through repeated institutional washing
Blazers & Jackets Shoulder Pads + Tatron Defined chest, lapel and shoulder structure with a smooth interior lining
Trousers & Bottomwear Pocketing Cloth + Belt Roll & Placket (Patti) Tear-resistant pocket bags and firm waistband/placket support
Caps & Headwear Buckram Fabric Rigid, shape-holding structure for crown and peak
Industrial Garments Pocketing Cloth + Fusible Interlining Heavy-duty construction built for daily abrasion, not occasional wear
Fashion & Design-Led Garments Buckram, Fusible Interlining or EVA Foam Structure level set by design intent — soft drape to rigid silhouette
Activewear & Elasticated Waistbands Woven & Knitted Elastics Stretch recovery in waistbands, cuffs and closures through repeated wash cycles
Jacket & Blazer Linings Tatron Smooth, lightweight interior lining that doesn't add bulk or trap heat
Adjustable Closures & Bags Velcro Tape Reusable hook-and-loop fastening for waistbands, plackets, cuffs and bag flaps
