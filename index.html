Who We Are

A Delhi Manufacturer Garment Buyers Have Depended On Since 1977

Jai Baba Nanak Cloth Trading Co. started as a small cloth trading counter in Delhi and grew, order by order, into a full-scale interlining manufacturer. We never stopped doing the two things that built the business: answering the phone ourselves, and shipping fabric that matches the sample we sent.

Today we manufacture, supply, wholesale, trade and export interlining fabrics — buckram, fusible interlining, pocketing cloth, readymade collar & cuff, belt roll & placket (patti), shoulder pads and EVA foam — to garment units, buying houses and export merchants who cannot afford a bad batch holding up a shipment. Buckram is supplied just as readily to wholesalers and traders as it is to garment manufacturers, on the same direct, no-middleman terms.

  • Family-run business, now in its next generation of leadership
  • Consistent width, weight and bonding across every dispatch
  • Direct manufacturer pricing — no middleman markup
