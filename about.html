Our Story

Built Counter By Counter, Order By Order

Jai Baba Nanak Cloth Trading Co. began in 1977 as a small cloth trading counter in Delhi. There was no factory behind it at first — just a family willing to source fabric honestly and deliver what was promised, on the day it was promised.

Over the decades, that trading counter grew into in-house manufacturing. Machines were added as demand grew, product lines were built one at a time, and the business moved from simply trading cloth to producing it — buckram, fusible interlining, pocketing cloth, readymade collar & cuff, belt roll & placket (patti), shoulder pads and EVA foam. Buckram alone moves through every channel we operate — manufactured for garment units and supplied in bulk to wholesalers and traders alike.

The company remains family-run to this day. It is now guided by the next generation of the Chawla family, with Mr. Sahil Chawla the person garment manufacturers and buying houses deal with directly for orders, specifications and dispatch.

  • Family-owned since 1977, now in its next generation of leadership
  • Grew from trading to full in-house manufacturing

Manufacturing Legacy

A Factory Floor Built Up Over Decades, Not Overnight

Our manufacturing capability wasn't set up in a single investment — it was added to gradually, decade after decade, as the business earned the right to grow. Each machine, each process checkpoint, was added because a buyer needed it, not because it looked good on paper.

That gradual build-up is why our manufacturing process is as stable as it is today. Nothing on our floor is untested at scale, and every product category we run has years of production history behind it.
