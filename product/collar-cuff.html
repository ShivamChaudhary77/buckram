Overview

A Step Up In Precision From General-Purpose Fusible

Standard fusible interlining works across a wide range of garment parts. Readymade collar & cuff is a different job — it is cut and calibrated specifically for the fold, roll and edge of a shirt collar and cuff, where any inconsistency shows up immediately on a finished garment.

We produce this category with tighter control over stiffness and edge behaviour than our general-purpose fusible line, because a collar has to do something a flat panel doesn't: roll cleanly at the fold, hold that roll after fusing, and keep a sharp edge through the iron, the wash and the wear.

  • Calibrated specifically for collar roll and cuff edge structure
  • Supplied readymade as die-cut, ready-to-fuse panels — or as roll form for your own cutting room
  • Consistent fuse quality across the full production run
