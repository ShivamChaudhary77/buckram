Product Overview

Built For Belt And Placket Lines That Can't Afford Width Variance

Belt roll & placket (patti) is a firm-bodied interlining fabric that we cut and roll to specific widths before it ever reaches your cutting table. Where general-purpose interlining is sold as bulk yardage and cut down at the buyer's end, this category arrives pre-sized — so every metre that comes off the roll matches the width your belt or placket design calls for.

That width consistency matters more on a belt or placket line than almost anywhere else in garment production. A soft fabric belt gets its shape entirely from the interlining inside it, and a trouser or shirt placket needs the same firm, even strip behind it to sit flat and stitch straight. If the roll width drifts even a few millimetres across a batch, the finished belts and plackets drift with it — and a procurement team ends up sorting rejects instead of shipping.

We manufacture belt roll & placket (patti) for fabric belts, waist ties, sash belts, uniform belts and trouser or shirt plackets, supplying garment units and buying houses that run belt and placket assembly as a dedicated production line, not a side task.

Product Features

What Makes Belt Roll & Placket (Patti) Different From Cut-To-Size Yardage

  • Firm body that gives a soft belt or placket its structure and consistent shape
  • Cut and rolled to precise, repeatable roll widths, not trimmed on-site
  • Stable edges that hold together through belt and placket assembly without fraying

Because the width is set at our end during production, your belt-line and placket-line operators work from a roll that's already correct — no re-measuring, no trimming station, no width checks before assembly.
