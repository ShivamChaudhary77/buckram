Product Overview

A Foam Sheet That Does Two Jobs Well

EVA foam is a closed-cell foam sheeting that garment manufacturers use in two distinct ways — as a padding and structure component built into a finished garment, and as a protective insert that keeps goods from shifting or getting crushed in transit. We supply it as sheets or rolls, cut to the thickness and firmness the application calls for.

Because the same base material serves both roles, buying houses that need padding inserts for one order and packaging inserts for another can source both from a single supplier instead of managing two separate vendors.

  • Supplied as cut sheets or rolls, depending on your production line
  • Soft to firm grades available for different padding requirements
  • Die-cuttable to shape for repeat-pattern component orders
