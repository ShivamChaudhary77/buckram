Product Overview

A Pocket That Fails Before the Garment Does Is a Returned Garment

Pocketing cloth doesn't get noticed when it works — it only gets noticed when it splits at the corner, frays at the seam or lets a coin pocket sag after a few washes. Because pockets take constant hand contact, friction from wallets and keys, and the same wash cycles as the rest of the garment, the fabric behind them has to outlast the shell fabric it's stitched into, not just match it.

We manufacture pocketing cloth as a tightly woven, stable-construction fabric in cotton, poly-cotton and polyester options, supplied in standard rolls or custom widths to match your cutting layout. It's built for one job: hold its shape and its seams through the full working life of the garment.

  • Tight, stable weave resistant to tearing and seam-slip
  • Smooth hand-feel suited to constant hand and pocket contact
  • Consistent width and weight across every production batch
