Product Overview

Structure That Sets Behind Every Clean Shoulder Line

Shoulder pads are moulded inserts placed inside a jacket, blazer or coat to build and hold the shoulder shape the outer fabric alone cannot hold on its own. They sit at the point of the garment a buyer's eye goes to first — a jacket can be well cut everywhere else and still look wrong if the pad underneath is uneven or has gone flat.

Moulded consistency is the whole job here. A pair of pads that differ even slightly in curve, thickness or density will throw off the left-right symmetry of a finished garment, and that defect only shows up after the jacket is assembled — by which point the cost of the mistake has multiplied. We mould every pad to the same shape and density profile, run after run, so a left pad and right pad match, and so pad 5,000 in an order matches pad 1.
