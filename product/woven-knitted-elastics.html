Product Overview

Elastic That Still Recovers After The Hundredth Wash

An elastic tape that stretches well off the roll but goes slack after a season of washing hasn't done its job — it's simply failed slower. We manufacture woven and knitted elastic tape built around recovery, not just initial stretch, so a waistband holds its grip and a cuff holds its shape through the full working life of the garment.

Woven and knitted construction serve different jobs: woven elastic holds a firmer, more stable width for structured waistbands, while knitted elastic gives a softer hand and better stretch-in-both-directions for cuffs and close-to-skin applications. We produce both, so you're not forced to compromise one job for the other.

  • Consistent stretch recovery, wash after wash
  • Woven and knitted construction available from the same supplier
  • Standard and custom widths, manufactured in-house in Delhi
