Product Overview

Fabric Built To Hold Its Shape, Not Just Its Colour

Buckram is a sized or resin-treated fabric produced specifically for stiffness rather than drape. Where most interlining is chosen for how it bends, buckram is chosen for how well it resists bending — it is the material behind a cap peak that stays flat, a belt that doesn't fold under its own weight, or a box-style bag panel that keeps its edges square.

We manufacture buckram on a cotton or blended base, treated to a firm, stable hand-feel that holds up through cutting, stitching and the finished life of the garment component. Every roll is produced to a consistent stiffness so a pattern piece cut from metre 5 behaves the same as one cut from metre 500.

Where It's Used

Applications

Buckram goes into any garment component or accessory that needs to hold a fixed shape rather than fall or drape.

Caps & Cap Peaks

Front panel and peak stiffening for structured caps that hold a fixed profile.

Belts

Core stiffening for fabric belts that need to stay firm and uncrushed.

Structured Bags & Boxes

Panel and edge support for structured bag and box-style components.

Waistband Stiffening

Added body at the waistband for a cleaner, uncollapsed finish.

Available Options

Buckram Variants

Choose a stiffness grade based on how much rigidity your component needs.

Light Stiffness Grade

A softer buckram for components that need body without full rigidity — useful where some hand-flex is acceptable.

Medium Stiffness Grade

Our most commonly ordered grade, balancing firm shape-holding with workable cutting and stitching behaviour.

Heavy Stiffness Grade

Maximum rigidity for components like cap peaks and box panels that must hold a fixed profile under pressure.

Custom Width Rolls

Any of the above grades cut to a width matching your pattern layout, on confirmed bulk quantities.
