Product Overview

A Fastener That Still Grips After Thousands Of Uses

Velcro tape is a two-part hook-and-loop fastening system — one woven strip of fine hooks, one of soft loops — that grips on contact and releases cleanly on demand, without buttons, zips or snaps. It's a reusable closure, so the part that matters most is what happens after the first hundred uses, not just the first one.

We manufacture sew-on hook and loop tape to a consistent grip strength across the roll, so a waistband or placket closes with the same hold on use one thousand as it did on use one. Both the hook and loop strips are supplied together, cut and matched to the width your application calls for.

  • Consistent grip strength across thousands of open-close cycles
  • Sew-on hook and loop strips supplied matched and ready to stitch
  • Standard and custom widths, manufactured in-house in Delhi
